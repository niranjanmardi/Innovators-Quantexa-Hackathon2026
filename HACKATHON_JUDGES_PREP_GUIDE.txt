1. WHAT IS THIS PROJECT? (Simple Overview)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QuantPlatform is an institutional-grade Quantitative Research and Trading 
Strategy Platform.

In simple terms:
It is a web application where financial analysts and traders can:
1. Track real-time prices for stocks, crypto, indices, and gold.
2. Test algorithmic trading strategies against real historical data to see if 
   they actually make money before risking real capital.
3. Audit whether a trading strategy is realistic or just lucky/fake.
4. Read real-time financial market news linked to each asset.

The Real-World Problem We Solve:
Most online trading tools show "backtests" that look amazing on paper, but when 
people trade them with real money, they lose everything. That happens because 
standard tools ignore trading fees, use fake data, or test overfitted strategies. 
QuantPlatform solves this by adding real fees, slippage, and an automated 
"Trust Engine" to verify if a strategy is genuinely reliable.

2. HOW EACH PAGE WORKS (What to Show & What to Say)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PAGE 1: DASHBOARD
-----------------
What it is: 
The home screen giving a quick bird's-eye view of global markets.

What to show:
- Live price cards for major benchmarks: S&P 500 (SPY), Nasdaq (QQQ), 
  Bitcoin (BTC-USD), and Gold (GLD).
- Daily percentage gain/loss and trading volumes.
- Latest breaking financial news headlines in the sidebar.

What to say to the judges:
"Our dashboard gives researchers immediate macro visibility. We stream live 
market indices alongside breaking news so analysts can understand market 
sentiment at a glance."


PAGE 2: ASSET ANALYSIS
----------------------
What it is: 
A deep-dive analysis page for any individual stock or cryptocurrency.

What to show:
- Search for any ticker (like AAPL, NVDA, TSLA, BTC-USD).
- The interactive price chart with moving average trendlines (SMA and EMA).
- Annualized historical volatility showing how risky or wild the price swings are.
- Filtered news headlines specifically mentioning that asset.

What to say to the judges:
"In Asset Analysis, a user can enter any stock or crypto ticker. The platform 
fetches real historical data, calculates moving averages, measures price 
volatility over 30-day windows, and automatically brings up news articles 
affecting that specific asset."


PAGE 3: CORRELATION LAB
-----------------------
What it is: 
A risk management tool that checks how different assets move together.

What to show:
- Select multiple assets (for example: Tech stocks, Crypto, and Gold).
- The color-coded heatmap showing numbers between -1.0 and +1.0.
  * +1.0 means they move in the exact same direction.
  * 0.0 means they have no relationship.
  * -1.0 means when one goes up, the other goes down.

What to say to the judges:
"The Correlation Lab helps investors build balanced portfolios. For example, 
if you hold both Bitcoin and NVIDIA, our heatmap shows you how closely they 
move together, helping you add assets like Gold to protect yourself when the 
market drops."


PAGE 4: STRATEGY LAB (The Main Star Feature)
--------------------------------------------
What it is: 
An algorithmic strategy simulator that tests whether a trading idea works.

What to show:
1. Choose an asset (e.g., Bitcoin or Apple).
2. Pick a trading strategy:
   - SMA Crossover (buys when short-term trend crosses above long-term trend)
   - EMA Trend (faster trend following)
   - Momentum (buys assets that have been rising strongly)
   - Mean Reversion (buys dips when prices are abnormally low, sells on bounce)
3. Enter initial cash (e.g., $10,000) and click "Run Backtest".

What the results show:
- Equity Curve: A chart comparing your strategy's profit against simply holding 
  the asset ("Buy & Hold Benchmark").
- Key Stats: Total profit, win rate percentage, and maximum drawdown (the biggest 
  drop your account suffered).
- Trust Score: A score grading whether the strategy is genuinely robust or just 
  overfitted to historical luck.
- Market Regime Breakdown: Shows how much money the strategy makes during Bull 
  markets versus Bear markets.

What to say to the judges:
"In the Strategy Lab, users can test 4 algorithmic trading strategies. Unlike 
basic simulators, we compare results against Buy & Hold, include real broker 
fees and slippage, and evaluate performance across both Bull and Bear markets."


PAGE 5: MARKET NEWS INTELLIGENCE
--------------------------------
What it is: 
A live news feed tracking macroeconomic and business developments.

What to show:
- Category tabs: Top Stories, Business & Finance, Technology, Crypto.
- Clean news cards with images, sources, and direct links to original articles.
- Search box to filter news by keywords.

What to say to the judges:
"Quantitative models cannot ignore breaking news. Our News Intelligence engine 
delivers real-time, categorized financial articles with a multi-tier fallback 
system to ensure researchers always have live market context."

3. TOP 5 HIGHLIGHTS THAT WILL IMPRESS THE JUDGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. THE TRUST ENGINE (Anti-Overfitting)
   Judges love risk management. Explain that our platform runs "sensitivity 
   tests". If changing a 20-day average to 19 days causes the strategy to crash, 
   our Trust Engine flags it as "fragile/overfitted" and lowers its trust score.

2. REALISTIC TRADING COSTS
   In the real world, brokers charge fees and prices slip during execution. 
   Our backtester deducts commissions and slippage so users see realistic profits, 
   not fantasy profits.

3. MARKET REGIME ANALYSIS
   A strategy might make 50% during a Bull market but lose 80% during a Bear 
   market. Our system automatically breaks down returns by market regime so 
   traders know when their strategy works best.

4. STRICT ZERO-MOCK-DATA POLICY
   We never use fake or fabricated numbers. All stock prices, crypto prices, 
   and news come from real live financial APIs (Yahoo Finance and NewsData).

5. AI AS AN EXPLAINER, NOT A CRYSTAL BALL
   We do not use AI to "guess tomorrow's stock price"—because that is dangerous 
   and unreliable. Instead, our AI acts as a smart financial analyst: it looks 
   at the computed numbers and explains in plain English why the strategy won 
   or lost.

4. THE TECHNOLOGY BEHIND IT (Simple Summary)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Frontend: Built using React and Tailwind CSS for a fast, modern user experience. 
  Hosted live on Vercel.
- Backend: Built using Python and FastAPI for fast numerical calculations using 
  Pandas and NumPy. Hosted live on Render.
- User Accounts: Secure login and user profiles using encrypted passwords (bcrypt) 
  and secure tokens (JWT).
- Reliability: Smart caching that saves API calls and prevents rate limits.
