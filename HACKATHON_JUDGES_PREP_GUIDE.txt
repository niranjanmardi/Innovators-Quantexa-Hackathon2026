1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE VALUE PROPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Project Name: QuantPlatform (Research OS)
Domain:       FinTech / Quantitative Finance & Decision Intelligence
Repository:   https://github.com/niranjanmardi/Innovators-Quantexa-Hackathon2026

The Core Problem:
- 90% of retail algorithmic backtests appear profitable historically but fail in 
  live execution due to:
  1. Overfitting (tuning parameters to historical noise)
  2. Look-ahead bias (using future information in past decisions)
  3. Zero-slippage and zero-commission assumptions
  4. "Black-box" predictions that cannot be explained or audited.

The QuantPlatform Solution:
- An institutional-grade quantitative research and strategy verification platform.
- It acts as an algorithmic trust auditor: verifying parameter robustness, 
  preventing look-ahead leakage, segmenting returns across market regimes, 
  and stress-testing strategies against historic flash crashes.

Core Design Principles:
1. Strict Zero-Mock Policy: Only real historical/live market feeds (Yahoo Finance).
2. Explainability Over Prediction: AI explains actual computed quantitative statistics 
   (Sharpe, Max Drawdown, Regime Sensitivity)—it does NOT predict future prices.
3. Realistic Execution: Explicit deduction of commission costs and slippage basis points.

3. FEATURE-BY-FEATURE BREAKDOWN (PAGE BY PAGE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[1] DASHBOARD (Route: /)
- Real-time global market indices and benchmark assets (S&P 500, Nasdaq, BTC, Gold).
- Daily percentage change, price spreads, and volume tracking.
- Sidebar streaming live categorized financial news headlines.

[2] ASSET ANALYSIS (Route: /asset)
- User Input: Asset ticker symbol (e.g., AAPL, NVDA, BTC-USD, GLD) + date range.
- Technical Overlays:
  * Simple Moving Averages (20-day, 50-day, 200-day SMA).
  * Exponential Moving Averages (EMA).
- Statistical Computations:
  * Daily Simple Returns.
  * 30-day Rolling Historical Volatility (annualized on 252-day trading basis).
  * Asset-specific news feed correlation.

[3] CORRELATION LAB (Route: /correlation)
- Purpose: Multi-asset diversification and portfolio risk concentration analysis.
- Logic:
  1. Synchronizes trading calendars across different asset types (e.g. equities 
     trade 252 days/yr vs. crypto 365 days/yr) using inner-join timestamp alignment.
  2. Computes the Pearson Correlation Matrix across all selected assets.
- Visualization: Interactive color-coded heatmap (-1.0 to +1.0).

[4] STRATEGY LAB (Route: /strategy) - [THE CORE QUANT ENGINE]
Simulates and benchmarks 4 algorithmic trading strategies:
1. SMA Crossover: Fast SMA crossing Slow SMA to capture macro trends.
2. EMA Trend: Exponentially-weighted moving average to reduce reaction lag.
3. Momentum (RSI / Rate of Change): Long positions on rolling price velocity.
4. Mean Reversion: Statistical arbitrage buying dips below standard deviation bands 
   and exiting upon return to the mean.

Key Backtest Capabilities:
- Benchmark Comparison: Direct comparison against Buy & Hold on identical timeframe.
- Friction Modeling: Realistic trade commissions ($/trade) and slippage (basis points).
- Detailed Metrics: Total Return, Annualized Return, Sharpe Ratio, Max Drawdown, 
  Win/Loss Ratio, Profit Factor, Largest Losing Streak.

[5] TRUST ENGINE & INTEGRITY LAYER
- Parameter Sensitivity Check: Perturbs hyperparameters by +/- 10% to +/- 20%. 
  If a strategy collapses upon small parameter changes, it is flagged as FRAGILE/OVERFIT.
- Look-Ahead Bias Prevention: Signals computed at bar t close strictly execute at 
  bar t+1 open.
- Turnover Sanity Check: Measures trading frequency to ensure transaction costs 
  do not consume total profits.
- Algorithmic Trust Score (0 to 100): Composite score grading real-world viability.

[6] REGIME ENGINE & STRESS-TEST LAB
- Regime Classification: Partitions historical backtest periods into 4 economic regimes:
  1. Bull Market (Upward trend + Low Volatility)
  2. Bear Market (Downward trend + High Volatility)
  3. Choppy / Sideways (Low trend + Range-bound)
  4. Crisis Shock (High Volatility Spikes)
- Stress-Test Scenarios: Simulates historic black swan events (March 2020 Flash Crash, 
  2022 Tech Sell-Off, 3x Slippage Spikes) to test survival.

[7] MARKET NEWS INTELLIGENCE (Route: /news)
- Dual-Provider Live Architecture:
  * Primary: NewsData.io API (rate-limited, credit budget tracking).
  * Secondary Fallback: Real-time Yahoo Finance news stream (no API key required).
- Features: Article deduplication, HTTPS link validation, image extraction, 
  category filtering (Tech, Business, Crypto, Macro).

4. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS (CHEAT SHEET TO MEMORIZE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Daily Simple Return:
   R_t = (P_t - P_{t-1}) / P_{t-1}

2. Cumulative Return:
   CumRet_t = Product_{i=1}^t (1 + R_i) - 1

3. Annualized Historical Volatility:
   sigma_daily = sqrt( (1 / (N - 1)) * Sum_{t=1}^N (R_t - Mean_R)^2 )
   sigma_annual = sigma_daily * sqrt(252)   [Note: Uses sqrt(365) for Crypto]

4. Annualized Sharpe Ratio:
   Sharpe = ( (Mean_R - R_f) / sigma_excess ) * sqrt(252)
   (Where R_f is the risk-free rate, defaulted to 0.0 for conservative baseline)

5. Running Peak & Drawdown Series:
   Peak_t = Max_{i <= t} (Portfolio_Value_i)
   DD_t   = (Portfolio_Value_t - Peak_t) / Peak_t
   Max Drawdown (MDD) = Min_t (DD_t)

6. Pearson Correlation Coefficient:
   rho_{X,Y} = Sum( (X_i - Mean_X) * (Y_i - Mean_Y) ) /
               sqrt( Sum(X_i - Mean_X)^2 * Sum(Y_i - Mean_Y)^2 )

5. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS BY JUDGES & WINNING ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1: "Does your AI predict stock prices?"
A1: "No. In professional quantitative finance, using generative LLMs to predict 
    future prices is statistically invalid and prone to hallucination. Our AI 
    layer acts strictly as a Quantitative Analyst and Explainer: it ingests real 
    computed statistics (Sharpe, Drawdown, Regime breakdowns) and translates 
    complex quantitative telemetry into actionable, auditable risk reports."

Q2: "How do you protect against look-ahead bias in backtests?"
A2: "In our BacktestEngine, signals generated at the close of time t are strictly 
    executed at the opening price of time t+1. We also run automated timestamp 
    assertions to ensure future data cannot contaminate historical state."

Q3: "How does your platform prevent overfitting?"
A3: "Through our TrustEngine. We run automated parameter sensitivity sweeps (+/- 10% 
    to +/- 20%). If a strategy is only profitable at an exact 20-day parameter and 
    crashes at 18 or 22 days, our TrustEngine flags it as overfitted and reduces 
    its Trust Score."

Q4: "How do you handle different trading calendars in the Correlation Lab?"
A4: "Equities trade approximately 252 days per year during market hours, whereas 
    cryptocurrencies trade 24/7 across 365 days. Our QuantEngine performs an 
    inner-join timestamp alignment on daily closing prices so correlations are 
    calculated strictly on concurrent dates."

Q5: "How does your architecture handle rate limits and outages in production?"
A5: "We built a resilient multi-tier caching service (In-Memory + Redis support) 
    with a daily credit budget manager. For instance, our News Intelligence engine 
    automatically falls back to Yahoo Finance streaming if NewsData.io quotas 
    are reached, ensuring zero downtime and zero blank screens."

Q6: "Why is this solution relevant to Quantexa?"
A6: "Quantexa's core mission is Decision Intelligence: replacing black-box 
    ambiguity with transparent, connected, and trusted data. QuantPlatform applies 
    that exact principle to algorithmic finance by turning opaque backtests into 
    auditable, explainable, and stress-tested decision systems."
